Facilitate rule debugging:

1. Add a bogus target (e.g. /t/) to the /Makefile/.
2. Call the /Makefile/ with the following command: =make -p t > debug-output.txt=.

Looking at the generated rules is especially helpful when rules are
dynamically generated by combining the [[https://www.gnu.org/software/make/manual/html_node/Call-Function.html][Call]] and [[https://www.gnu.org/software/make/manual/html_node/Eval-Function.html][Eval]] functions.
