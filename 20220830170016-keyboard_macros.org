Main [[https://www.gnu.org/software/emacs/manual/html_node/emacs/Basic-Keyboard-Macro.html][documentation]].  Excellent [[https://www.masteringemacs.org/article/keyboard-macros-are-misunderstood][article.]]

Start recording a macro:  {C-x (}  or {F3}

Stop recording a macro:   {C-x )}  or {F4}

Execution a macro:        {C-x e}

Name a macro:             {C-x C-k n}

Bind a macro:             {C-x C-k b}
