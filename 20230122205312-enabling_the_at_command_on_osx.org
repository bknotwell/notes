#+title: Enabling the at command on OSX
#+OPTIONS: ^:nil author:nil html-postamble:nil
#+SETUPFILE: ./latexcss.theme

You must enable the =atrun= command:
   =sudo launchctl load -w /System/Library/LaunchDaemons/com.apple.atrun.plist=

=man atrun= for slightly more information.

You also need to setup =/usr/libexec/atrun= to have /Full Disk Access/ by
going to the /Security & Privacy/ area in settings and adding it.  *NB:*
to access the =/usr/libexec= directory, you need to use the
{Cmd-Shift-G} key chord to open a dialog.
