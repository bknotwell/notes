#+title: Symmetric Encryption Example
#+OPTIONS: ^:nil author:nil html-postamble:nil
#+SETUPFILE: ./latexcss.theme

This is an easy example showing how to do symmetric encryption with [[https://openssl.org][OpenSSL]].

Generate a key:

#+begin_src sh
openssl enc -aes-256-cbc -pass env:PASSPHRASE -P -md sha256 -iter 2000000 -pbkdf2 > keyfile
#+end_src

*NB:* the =-iter 2000000 -pbdkdf2= arguments increase the security of the
=PASSPHRASE=. 

Test the encryption and decryption of a short phrase:

#+begin_src sh
[ "$(echo 'hello world' | openssl enc -aes-256-cbc -a -K  $(sed '/^key=/!d;s/^.*=//' keyfile) -iv $(sed '/^iv =/!d;s/^.*=//' keyfile) |  openssl enc -aes-256-cbc -d -a -K  $(sed '/^key=/!d;s/^.*=//' keyfile) -iv $(sed '/^iv =/!d;s/^.*=//' keyfile))" = 'hello world' ]
#+end_src
