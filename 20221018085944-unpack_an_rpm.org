RPMs are just [[https://pubs.opengroup.org/onlinepubs/7908799/xcu/cpio.html][cpio]] files with some additional information.  Ripping
them apart is simple with the[[https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man8/rpm2cpio.8.html][ rpm2cpio]] utility:

#+begin_src sh
rpm2cpio rpmfile | cpio -id
#+end_src
