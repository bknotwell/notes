#+title: Switch orgmode table to table-mode
#+OPTIONS: ^:nil author:nil html-postamble:nil
#+SETUPFILE: ./latexcss.theme

Orgmode tables have a rudimentary formatting and won't do multi-line
tables.  If you switch to /table-mode/, you have more editing options
available:

1. {Ctrl-c ~} from a point in the table to switch the mode.
2. {Ctrl-c '} from a point in the table to enter table-mode.

Rendering table-mode tables isn't as well-supported so exports may
require additional editing.
