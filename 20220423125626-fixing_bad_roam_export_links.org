If =org-publish= fails with an /Unable to resolve link/ failure, try
[[https://dev.to/devteam/resolving-an-unable-to-resolve-link-error-for-org-mode-in-emacs-2n1f][fixing]] the issue by doing the following:

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (progn (org-id-update-id-locations)
  ;; Note: you may need `org-roam-db-clear-all'
  ;; followed by `org-roam-db-sync'
         (org-roam-db-sync)
         (org-roam-update-org-id-locations))
#+end_src

After executing the above functions, publishing should work again.
