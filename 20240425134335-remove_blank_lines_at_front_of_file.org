A one-liner to remove blank lines at the front of a file:

#+begin_src sh
# delete the blank lines until we hit one that isn't and 
# then loop through the rest of the file branching back to
# :a label.
sed -e '/^$/d;:a' -e 'n;ba' filename
#+end_src

The above code's portable across POSIX and GNU sed.
